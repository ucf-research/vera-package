The VERA Telemetry Replay sample showcases how to "replay" a participant's telemetry session in the Unity editor, allowing you to see an accurate recording of the participant's session.

To use this sample, open the VERATelemetryReplay scene, and click on the "Replays" game object. Drag-and-drop a telemetry file of your choosing onto the "Telemetry File" field of the "VERA Telemetry Replay" component, and click play - a virtual Quest headset and controllers should replay the participant's movements. While the scene is running, you can find pause/play and scrubbing tools in the VERA Telemetry Replay component's inspector view. 

See the VERA documentation page on the sample for more information: https://vera-xr.io/static/docs/sections/advanced-features/telemetry_replay.html
